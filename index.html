About Sohal Electrical Works

A trusted local electrical business built on quality service, honest pricing, and long-term customer relationships.

Powering Homes & Industries Since 2008

Founded near Truck Union, Sirhind-Morinda Road, Sohal Electrical Works started as a trusted local electrical supply store and has grown into a reliable name across Punjab for quality electrical products and honest customer service.

We specialize in supplying electrical fittings, industrial materials, switches, pipes, wires, lighting products, junction boxes, and heavy-duty electrical accessories for homes, shops, factories, and contractors.
